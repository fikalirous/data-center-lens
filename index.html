The Cloud's Footprint

Mapping the world's data center infrastructure against each nation's ecological footprint, biocapacity, and digital economy.

About this project

This project maps where the internet's physical infrastructure — data centers and internet exchange points — actually sits, and asks a harder question: what does hosting that infrastructure cost the planet, and who is positioned to benefit from it? Every country in this dataset carries three angles: how much digital infrastructure it hosts, whether its ecological footprint outstrips what its own land and sea can regenerate, and how developed its digital economy is. The interactive map below lets you explore all three at once.

How it works

Drag any of the three lens circles across the map. Each lens aggregates the data points beneath it live into a bar chart. Overlap two or three lenses on the same region to compare all three angles for one place at once.
